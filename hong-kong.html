Hong Kong is where the exchange really began — the humidity, the neon, the trains that never stopped moving. I remember buying my camera here, from a market stall I've since walked past a hundred times without finding again. Everything in this city felt loud and alive, even on the nights I spent standing still with a lens in my hand.
