## Client Brief

**Client:** Alix, one of my best friend, who I met on exchange in Hong Kong. A photographer who fell in love with the city and decided to buy a disposable camera and shoot her travels to have a collection and a memory of her time abroad. 

**Purpose:** This website is to have an interactive collection of Alix's photography so that her trips are organized in a creative way. 

**Audience:** Friends, family, and people from Alex's exchange, plus anyone she sends the link to. She wants the audience to live this sort of colorful experience. She wants them to have fun interacting with the website. 


**Key action:** Click a block. The puzzle pieces is the navigation a visitor should pick a color, land inside a trip, look through the photos, and come back for another.

**Pages needed:**

*Home:* A full puzzle, which are interlocking rectangles in flat, saturated colors and irregular sizes, so they kind of read as this very colorful puzzle. There are around 12 blocks, but 5 of them carry a photograph and a link to the trip page. There would be a header on top of the blocks, which would be a navigation page, and there would be an About Me.

*Individual Trip Page:* Five trip pages, one per destination, all built from the same template: a solid color panel on one side holding the trip title and a written description, a large hero photograph beside it, then a vertical scrolling set of that trip's photos below

*About Me:* Alix's story about the love of movies and film that started it, the Instagram accounts that pulled her further in, the camera she bought secondhand at a market stall in Hong Kong, and facts from the exchange itself.

**Content status:** 
Have: Alex's photographs from the exchange
Need: 
- hero photograph for each trip, and the one photo that sits in each homepage block
- Her About text, plus the camera's make and model
- Confirmation she's happy for the photos to be public on the open web

**Style preferences:** colors, fonts, feel, from what they told you

**Inspiration sites:** We looked at a lot of sites for photographer-based individuals or creative-based individuals, and the one that stood out, which we're taking a lot of inspiration from, is Pierre Couture's portfolio, which is the main reference. However, she prefers puzzles to Tetris, so we changed that. The editorial aspect, once you click, is like a Zara website if you were to show the clothes, but it's basically a description, and then you see her photos.
